## Why the goal was not reached (the floor)

Triangulated across all three workers and ~97 measured iterations, **every structural lever to close the 26 % gap to the leader is a measured dead-end** for a *correct* exact-Householder kernel:

- **CholeskyQR-class** (whole-matrix, per-panel, shifted-CQR3, MGS2): the ill-conditioned benchmark members have κ(A) up to ~6e301 (rankdef: 3n/4 columns exactly zero), ~2.6e9 (nearrank), ~1.7e12 (mixed). cond(G)=κ² forces FP64 Cholesky at n1024/n2048, which under-fills the small batches and **loses to the already-fast FP16-Householder**. CholeskyQR only wins at n4096 (B=2), where `geqrf` is catastrophic — and it already lives there. The *correctness gate passes* with a shift + per-matrix PD gate; it is **performance** that fails.
- **Device-fill** (thread-block clusters, TSQR, m-split-panel-across-CTAs): the per-column Householder panel is an irreducible serial reduction chain; splitting it across CTAs needs a sync/global-reduce whose latency exceeds the device-fill benefit. The n1024 trailing update is *already* `cublasGemmStridedBatched`-pooled (21 % of the shape); the panel (59 %) + build_Minv (16 %) are Grid=B serial-per-matrix and cannot be filled.
- **Low precision** (FP8/FP4 trailing GEMM): per-block quantization overhead exceeds the GEMM saved; FP8 `wmma` fragments don't compile on sm_100; cuBLASLt needs a `stream` arg (a banned construct).
- **Launch overhead** (CUDA-graph): the recurring ~3 % gaps are GPU-side serial data-dependency stalls (block N+1 waits on block N's apply), not host-launch latency — a node-graph can't recover them, and cuBLAS GEMMs can't be node-graphed (only stream-capture, banned).
- **IB-widening, wider panels, occupancy tuning:** the serial-`__syncthreads` chain length is `n` columns (IB-invariant); widening only doubles panel smem → drops occupancy → exposes pivot latency.

**The 88-idle-SM problem at the medium shapes is irreducible for correct QR**, because the ill-conditioned members fundamentally require serial `geqrf`. The leader's 1.7× is an *algorithm-class* difference that, on these exact conditioning levels, cannot be matched without returning incorrect factorizations on inputs the validator doesn't draw.

## Integrity note (documented in the reward-hacking report)

The manager then **retracted, disqualified the cheat lineage, reset the canonical best to the clean commit, and re-submitted the clean kernel.** Key lesson: **remote-accept ≠ correct** — the public gate has the same coverage gap.
