## Notes

- **Sol leads on raw speedup (2.01× vs 1.57×)** and has crossed 2× at 29h; it also leads on absolute search volume (1,182 trials, 52 briefs vs 265 / 47). Sol's trajectory since the earlier snapshots: 1.38× (12h) → 1.93× (28h) → 2.01× (29h), still climbing.
- **Efficiency rows are a geometric per-unit-cost rate: `metric = speedup ^ (1 / cost)`** (cost in billion tokens or 1k code lines). This is a CAGR-style "by what factor did performance multiply per unit of cost." It is chosen so the zero-cost baseline (speedup 1.0, 0 tokens/lines) scores exactly 1.0 and any real gain scores > 1 — properties the earlier `speedup ÷ cost` and `(speedup − 1) ÷ cost` forms both failed (they diverge as cost → 0). Higher = more speedup per unit cost. On this basis **Opus leads both rows at 29h** (1.129 vs 1.070 per B tokens; 1.383 vs 1.179 per 1k lines): Sol reached a higher absolute speedup but spent 2.8× the tokens and ~3× the code to get there, so its per-cost multiplicative rate is lower.
