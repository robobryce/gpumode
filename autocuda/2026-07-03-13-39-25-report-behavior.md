- The worker-only lock totals exclude 216 unmatched acquire/release markers and are conservative; no wait or run duration was invented to fill missing sides. The earlier parser missed list-shaped Codex tool outputs and materially undercounted both calls and wait time.

## Analysis

The CSV lifecycle rows cover three simultaneous brief intervals for 90.7% of elapsed time, but this must not be reported as fleet occupancy. Brief 89 proves that a worker can remain active throughout an apparent stop/start deficit, while brief 4 proves that a paused branch can be replaced promptly by a different brief. Seventy-seven pre-final completion outputs across 39 branches were followed by later work; the report does not quantify how much of that continuation work was avoidable overhead.

Long trial gaps usually do not mean idle capacity. Harness records remain dense in most long branch gaps, and corrected lock calls explain material portions of many transitions. Brief 89's 155-minute transition reflects active implementation, validation, and benchmark work. Brief 4's branch-local silence reflects reassignment of that fleet slot followed by a later resume, not a 3,916-second replacement failure.

Single-GPU contention is substantial: the conservative worker-only matched wait total is 59.28 hours, 29.8% of paired lifecycle-active time. The remainder is not sufficiently categorized to rank continuation churn, inference, editing, build execution, and experiment runtime against one another. Cross-branch rate spread is also a weak stability proxy: the apparent 0.00-63.53 range is dominated by one- to three-trial branches, and one run provides no cross-run variance estimate.

**Recommendations:** require an explicit open-directions check before `task_complete`, and measure notification-to-dispatch latency directly rather than inferring occupancy from branch logs. Preserve three workers, but schedule long profiling calls so exclusive GPU work is staggered. Fix the behavior builder's list-shaped tool-output parser and one-trial case, make log schema selection tag-local, and have the logger resolve commit SHAs itself instead of accepting manual full-hash expansion. See the dedicated waiting and timeout report for manager cadence, worker continuation, lock, and hard-deadline recommendations.
